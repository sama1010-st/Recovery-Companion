Your Recovery, Your Strength.

Learn about post-surgery care, healthy habits, warning signs, and emotional well-being.

Recovery Guidelines

Simple information to support your recovery.

Gentle Movement

Follow the exercises recommended by your healthcare team. Start gently and avoid movements that cause pain.

Wound Care

Follow your medical team's instructions for keeping the surgical area clean and monitoring changes.

Medication Safety

Take medications exactly as prescribed. Do not change your dose or stop a medication without consulting your healthcare team.

Rest and Sleep

Give your body enough time to rest. Follow your doctor's advice about daily activities and returning to normal routines.

Healthy Nutrition

Maintain balanced meals and drink enough fluids, unless your healthcare team has given you different instructions.

Emotional Well-being

Recovery can be emotionally challenging. Talk to someone you trust and ask your healthcare team about available support.

Important Warning Signs

Know when to contact your healthcare team.

Pay Attention to These Changes

  • Increasing redness, warmth, or swelling around the wound.
  • New or worsening pain.
  • Unusual fluid, pus, or a bad smell from the wound.
  • Fever or chills.
  • New or increasing swelling in the arm.

Contact your healthcare team promptly if you notice these symptoms. Seek urgent medical care for severe symptoms, chest pain, or difficulty breathing.

Daily Recovery Tips

Small steps can make a difference.

Follow your personal recovery plan.
Attend your scheduled follow-up appointments.
Record your symptoms and daily progress.
Ask for help whenever you need it.
